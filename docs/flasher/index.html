Bambu Monitor

Firmware for the LilyGO T-Encoder-Pro that shows Bambu Lab printers over LAN MQTT.

  1. Connect the T-Encoder-Pro with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click install, pick the USB JTAG/serial debug unit port.
  3. After it reboots, join the BambuMonitor-XXXX Wi-Fi shown on the screen and enter your Wi-Fi and printer details.
Your browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Flashing needs HTTPS.

If the port doesn't appear: hold the knob while plugging the cable in (boot mode), then try again. Later updates can be installed over Wi-Fi from the device's web page.
